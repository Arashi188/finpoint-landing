Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Finpoint and how it works.

Is Finpoint free to use?

Yes! Finpoint offers a generous free tier with unlimited expense tracking, basic analytics, and up to 3 savings goals. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited goals, advanced analytics, and priority support.

How does the AI categorization work?

Our AI uses machine learning to automatically categorize your transactions based on merchant data, spending patterns, and your custom rules. It learns from your behavior over time for increasingly accurate results.

Is my financial data secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption and never store your login credentials. Finpoint is read-only with read-only access, and we are SOC 2 Type II certified.

Can I connect multiple bank accounts?

Yes, Finpoint supports connections with over 12,000 financial institutions across the US, UK, and EU. Link all your accounts for a unified view of your complete financial picture.

What platforms do you support?

Finpoint is available on web, iOS, and Android. All your data syncs seamlessly across devices in real-time, so you can manage your finances wherever you are.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from your account settings. There are no cancellation fees, and you'll continue to have access to your plan features until the end of your current billing cycle.
